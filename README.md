# SUSKOM '25 — Faculty of Computing Treasure Hunt

## Event Overview
We participated in a treasure hunt organised by the Faculty of Computing during **SUSKOM '25**, and it was a fun yet meaningful experience. The activity challenged us to think critically, solve problems, and work together as a team while exploring different tasks and clues.

## About the Event
**SUSKOM '25** is an engaging inter-university gaming event that brings together students to compete in various challenges and games. The treasure hunt component was designed to foster collaboration, strategic thinking, and problem-solving skills in a competitive yet enjoyable environment.

### Event Details
- **Organizer** — Faculty of Computing, Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM)
- **Event Type** — Treasure Hunt / Problem-Solving Challenge
- **Registration Period** — 1-10 December
- **Participating Universities** — Multiple institutions including UTM, PERSAKA, and others

## What We Experienced
- **Critical Thinking** — Decoded clues and solved puzzles to progress through the treasure hunt
- **Teamwork & Collaboration** — Worked together as a team to overcome challenges
- **Communication Skills** — Coordinated strategies and shared ideas with team members
- **Interactive Learning** — Engaged in a fun, gamified learning experience beyond the classroom

## Key Takeaways
This treasure hunt was a great way to:
- **Strengthen Teamwork** — Building stronger bonds with teammates through collaborative problem-solving
- **Enhance Communication** — Practicing effective communication under time and competitive pressure
- **Enjoy Learning** — Demonstrating that education and professional development can be engaging and interactive
- **Network** — Connecting with students from other universities and building professional relationships
